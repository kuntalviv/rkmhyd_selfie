Celebrate the message of Universal Brotherhood

On September 11, 1893, Swami Vivekananda delivered his historic address at the World's Parliament of Religions in Chicago, beginning with the unforgettable words “Sisters and Brothers of America.”

Join us in revisiting that message of harmony, acceptance and universal brotherhood.

Why September 11?

A message that still speaks to the world

Swami Vivekananda's Chicago address introduced millions to India's spiritual traditions and offered a powerful call for tolerance, acceptance and harmony among religions.

This experience invites you to listen to the address, reflect on its message, answer a few simple questions and receive a commemorative certificate.
